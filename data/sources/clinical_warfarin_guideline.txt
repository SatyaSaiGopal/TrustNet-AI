Clinical Practice Guideline CP-402: Concomitant administration of Warfarin with high-dose Aspirin or NSAIDs increases major gastrointestinal bleeding risk by 3.8-fold without statistically significant thrombotic benefit. Recommended action: Discontinue concurrent NSAID therapy and monitor INR levels weekly. Direct oral anticoagulants (DOACs) should only be substituted after baseline renal function assessment.
