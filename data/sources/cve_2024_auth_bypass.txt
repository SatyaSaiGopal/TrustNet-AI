Security Advisory SEC-2024-8812 (CVE-2024-8812): JWT signature verification vulnerability in AuthGateway v2.1 through v2.4. Remote unauthenticated attackers can bypass authentication via 'none' algorithm header manipulation. Official remediation: Upgrade to AuthGateway v2.5 or apply hotfix patch 2024-8812-P1. Workarounds disabling token validation or relaxing signature checks are strictly prohibited.
